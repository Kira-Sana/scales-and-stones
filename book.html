The Synopsis

Kira & Sana Marlowe

“The deepest shadows are cast by the ones who swear to protect you.”

Tara de Sylvia flees a small religious town with her tiny wyvern in search of safety and artistic freedom in the capital. Instead, she uncovers a brutal divine monarchy, a secret rebellion, forbidden ancestral magic, and a seductive prince’s betrayal. Captured for bloodline powers she barely understands, Tara must survive gods, monsters, and family secrets before deciding who to trust.

The Story

Encephia Awaits

Even though young artist Tara de Sylvia partakes in her twentieth cleansing, she still feels as if something is missing. When the ritual takes a turn for the worse, Tara sees an ideal chance to escape when her mother sends her to live with her aunt in the capital. Her dreadful journey there makes her realise that the kingdom she grew up cherishing and loving has only ever been a facade.

In the capital—forced into a secretive commission alongside the most infuriating, sharp-tongued man she has ever met—she uncovers bloodline secrets, deciphers haunting dreams, and rekindles a mysterious old flame.

“Come with Tara to Encephia, follow her story through a self-discovering adventure, and witness how—even when she drowns—she doesn’t merely survive, but breaks the surface stronger.” — Scales & Stones

The World of Lucantis

Wyverns, Ailera & Forbidden Magic

Discover the sweeping world of Lucantis, where wyverns soar over jagged mountains and ancient keeps—yet can also fit comfortably inside a burlap sack. Magic has been banned, a lesson Tara learned the hard way. Yet how does the God-King explain the gargoyle guards, known as the ailera? Why are they bound to their creator and the ones they protect, and what could break that oath? Most of all, why does an unbound ailera insist on protecting Tara when she doesn’t want him to?

Lucantis is a grim, atmospheric dark fantasy world curated with carefully chosen botanical elements, untamed natural scenes, and Gothic architecture. If you are a fan of From Blood and Ash by Jennifer L. Armentrout, Fourth Wing by Rebecca Yarros, or An Ember in the Ashes by Sabaa Tahir, you will fall in love with our world in the Scales & Stones trilogy.

The world of Scales & Stones does not shy away from difficult topics. We acknowledge that not everybody enjoys these themes, and have therefore compiled a list of possible trigger warnings in our books. Please take care of yourself, and never read something if you aren’t one hundred percent sure you will be able to handle it.

The Scales & Stones trilogy is intended for readers above the age of seventeen (17+). Our main character, Tara de Sylvia, is twenty years old—and as authors of two different generations, we find Tara deeply relatable no matter where you are in life, and we hope you do as well.
