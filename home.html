Debut Romantasy

By Kira & Sana Marlowe

“The deepest shadows are cast by the ones who swear to protect you.”

Tara de Sylvia flees a small religious town with her tiny wyvern in search of safety and artistic freedom in the capital. Instead, she uncovers a brutal divine monarchy, a secret rebellion, forbidden ancestral magic, and a seductive prince’s betrayal. Captured for bloodline powers she barely understands, Tara must survive gods, monsters, and family secrets before deciding who to trust.
